# Indian Food Composition & Nutritional Science

## Lentils & Pulses (Dals)
- **Yellow Toor / Arhar Dal**: Staple Indian lentil. High in complex carbohydrates, plant-based protein (approx 22g protein per 100g raw, 6.8g per 100g cooked), and folic acid (vitamin B9). Folic acid is critical for cellular division and preventing neural tube defects.
- **Moong Dal (Split & Whole Green Gram)**: Highly digestible, hypoallergenic, and ideal during illness, recovery, or weaning. Moong dal sprouts have double the Vitamin C and bioavailable iron compared to dry seeds due to enzymatic germination.
- **Urad Dal (Black Gram)**: Dense in magnesium and potassium. High fiber helps insulin sensitivity. When combined with rice and fermented (as in Idli and Dosa batter), the phytate inhibitors decrease, allowing higher bioavailability of calcium and zinc.
- **Rajma (Kidney Beans) & Chole (Chickpeas)**: Exceptional sources of soluble fiber (beta-glucan and resistant starch). Resistant starch acts as a prebiotic in the colon, fermenting into short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) like butyrate, which protects gut mucosal integrity and reduces LDL cholesterol.

## Indian Breads & Grains
- **Whole Wheat Roti / Chapati**: Made from stone-ground whole wheat atta. Contains bran, germ, and endosperm. A medium 40g roti contains approximately 3.6g protein, 22g complex carbohydrates, and 3.8g fiber. Stone-ground milling preserves wheat germ oil containing Vitamin E.
- **Millets (Shree Anna - Ragi, Jowar, Bajra)**:
  - **Ragi (Finger Millet)**: The highest calcium content of any cereal (approx 344mg per 100g, over 30x higher than rice). Essential for bone mineral density, osteopenia prevention, and lactose-intolerant individuals.
  - **Bajra (Pearl Millet)**: Very high in iron and phosphorus. Promotes body warmth and sustained satiety during cooler seasons.
  - **Jowar (Sorghum)**: Gluten-free, rich in phenolic flavonoids and anthocyanins that combat oxidative inflammation.

## Dairy & Vegetarian Protein Powerhouses
- **Paneer (Indian Cottage Cheese)**: Prepared by coagulating milk with lemon or vinegar. 100g of paneer delivers approx 18g of complete casein protein and 21g of dairy fat. Casein digests slowly over 5-7 hours, releasing a continuous stream of branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs), making paneer ideal for evening consumption to prevent nocturnal muscle breakdown.
- **Dahi / Curd**: Contains living probiotic cultures (Lactobacillus acidophilus and Streptococcus thermophilus) that reinforce the gut microbiome, enhance lactose tolerance, and improve synthesis of biotin and Vitamin K2.
